Privacy Policy

Series Expense · Last updated 10 September 2026

Series Expense is a personal expense tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written to describe what the app actually does, not to reserve rights we don't use.

What we collect

  • An anonymized Apple ID. When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a unique identifier for you that is specific to this app. It is not your Apple username, your name, or anything that identifies you outside this app.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to share it. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your real address and use a private relay instead. Either choice works, and if you hide it we never see the real one.
  • The expenses you enter — amount, name, category, and date, plus repeat settings if you make an expense recurring.
  • Your categories and budgets, so they follow you to a new device or survive reinstalling the app.

We do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any browsing or usage history.

What stays only on your phone

  • Reminder settings — the daily notification times you pick are stored on the device and scheduled by iOS. No reminder ever passes through our server.
  • Home screen widget data — today's and this month's totals are written to a private storage area shared between the app and its widget, on your device only.
  • Your sign-in token, stored in the iOS Keychain.

Where your data is stored

Expenses, categories, and budgets are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway, located in the United States. The connection is encrypted. As the developer I can technically see expense records in that database, but they are linked only to an anonymized Apple ID, so there is no name or readable email attached to them.

Crash and error reporting

The app and its server send crash and error reports to Sentry, so problems can be found and fixed. These reports are deliberately limited: request bodies, headers, and web addresses' query parameters are removed before the report is sent, which means the contents of your expenses and the names of your categories are not included. A report contains the error itself, where in the code it happened, and basic device and app version information. Error reporting is disabled during development.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app: tap the gear icon on the Add screen, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every expense attached to it from the database. It cannot be undone, and it does not require contacting us. Signing out instead leaves your data intact so you can sign back in.

Data retention

Your expenses are kept until you delete them individually or delete your account. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond what is needed to operate the database. Crash reports are retained by Sentry under their own retention schedule, typically 90 days.

Children

Series Expense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how it is used, the date at the top of this page will be updated and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
