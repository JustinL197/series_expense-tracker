How do I delete my account?

Tap the gear icon at the top of the Add screen, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every expense attached to it. It cannot be undone. If you only want to sign out and keep your data, use Sign Out just above it.

I got a new phone. Where are my expenses?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used before and your expenses, categories, and budgets will be there. They're stored on your account, not on the device.

How do recurring expenses work?

Turn on Recurring when adding an expense and pick a pattern — weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, or yearly, with an optional end date. The Repeat sheet previews the next three dates so you can confirm it's right. Each new copy is added automatically on its due date; you can edit or delete any individual copy without affecting the rest.

How do I export my expenses?

On the Expenses screen, tap the share icon in the top right. This exports exactly what you're currently viewing — so if you've applied a filter or search, only those expenses are included. The file is a CSV that opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets, and you can AirDrop it, email it, or save it to Files.

My reminders aren't showing up

Reminders need notification permission. Open the iOS Settings app → Series Expense → Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Then re-open Settings in the app (the gear icon) and confirm the reminder toggle is enabled.

The widget isn't updating

The widget refreshes when you add, edit, or delete an expense while the app is open. If it looks stale, open the app once and it should catch up. Tapping the small eye icon on the widget hides the amounts — tap it again to show them.

Why does it ask to sign in again sometimes?

Sign-in sessions currently expire after a period of inactivity, which returns you to the login screen. Signing back in with Apple restores everything — no data is lost.

What is "Hide My Email"?

When signing in with Apple, you can share your real email or let Apple generate a private relay address instead. Either works. Your expenses are tied to an anonymized Apple ID rather than your email, so choosing to hide it costs you nothing.
